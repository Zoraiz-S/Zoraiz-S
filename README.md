### About Me

I build full-stack systems that use AI as a functional layer — not a demo. My focus is on LLM integration, multi-tenant SaaS architecture, and getting things into production.

Currently an **Implementation Specialist at [Maigrate](https://maigrate.com)**, deploying custom AI agents into client operations. Completing a **Professional Master of Computer Science at Illinois Institute of Technology** (graduated Fall 2025).
